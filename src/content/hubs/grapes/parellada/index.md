Parellada is one of the classic grapes of Cava, typically used to bring finesse, freshness, and a lighter profile to the blend. It is generally less weighty and less savory than Xarel·lo, and often less fruit-forward than many standalone still-wine grapes.

For beginners, Parellada is useful because it shows how sparkling wine blends can be shaped not just by intensity, but by lift, delicacy, and balance.

## At a glance

- **Region:** Catalonia, Spain
- **Typical style:** Light, fresh, delicate white grape
- **Known for:** Traditional Cava blends
- **Good for:** Understanding finesse and balance in sparkling wine
- **Similar to:** Other light blending grapes used for freshness rather than power

## Why it matters

Parellada helps complete the picture of the classic Cava blend and is important for understanding how different grapes contribute different roles in sparkling wine.
