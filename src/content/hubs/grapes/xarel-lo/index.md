Xarel·lo is one of the classic grapes of Cava and is especially important in higher-quality bottlings. It tends to bring body, acidity, herbal notes, and a more savory backbone than some of the other traditional Cava grapes.

For beginners, Xarel·lo matters because it helps explain why serious Cava can feel more structured and characterful than many people expect.

## At a glance

- **Region:** Catalonia, Spain
- **Typical style:** Fresh, structured white grape with savory notes
- **Known for:** Cava, especially quality-focused styles
- **Good for:** Understanding the identity of Spanish sparkling wine
- **Similar to:** Not directly similar, but often more structured than simple neutral sparkling bases

## Why it matters

Xarel·lo is one of the grapes that gives top Cava real personality.
