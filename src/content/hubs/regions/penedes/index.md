Penedès is one of Spain’s best-known wine regions and is strongly associated with Cava production. It produces still wines as well, but for many beginners it is most important as the center of quality-focused sparkling wine in Spain.

Studying Penedès helps explain the regional context behind Cava, especially the role of native grapes and modern producer identity.

## At a glance

- **Country:** Spain
- **Known for:** Cava and still wines
- **Main grapes:** Xarel·lo, Macabeo, Parellada
- **Style:** Sparkling and still wines
- **Good for:** Understanding Spanish sparkling wine

## Why it matters

Penedès is one of the most important regions for understanding serious Cava.
