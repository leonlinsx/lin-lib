Ribera del Duero is one of Spain’s most important red wine regions and is known for concentrated, structured, age-worthy wines based largely on Tempranillo. Compared with Rioja, the wines often feel darker, firmer, and more intense.

For beginners, Ribera del Duero is useful because it offers a strong regional contrast within Spanish red wine.

## At a glance

- **Country:** Spain
- **Known for:** Tempranillo-based red wines
- **Main grape:** Tempranillo
- **Style:** Structured, concentrated reds
- **Good for:** Comparing major Spanish red-wine regions

## Why it matters

Ribera del Duero is home to some of Spain’s most prestigious producers, including Vega Sicilia.
